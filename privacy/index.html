Privacy

Turnover Guard privacy information

Last updated: September 2026

Short version: Turnover Guard is designed to work locally. The plugin has no production telemetry, analytics backend, runtime AI, publisher license server or plugin account. It does not upload your media.

Plugin behavior

Turnover Guard reads the active Premiere sequence and relevant host observations locally to perform preflight checks and prepare a turnover. Some host observations may include source-media paths used temporarily for freshness comparison. The plugin does not use those paths to crawl your filesystem or upload files.

Output access is requested through supported UXP folder selection. The plugin uses the folder you choose to create the turnover output and report. It does not request unrestricted filesystem access, silently overwrite arbitrary existing files, or add a publisher-controlled online activation service.

What the plugin does not send

  • No production telemetry or analytics.
  • No customer media upload.
  • No remote AI requests.
  • No plugin account or publisher license-server traffic.
  • No automatic support upload.

Adobe Creative Cloud, Premiere Pro, operating-system services and marketplace/install services are separate products and may have their own network activity and privacy practices.

Reports and editorial information

Turnover reports can contain useful editorial context such as sequence, track, clip and output names. Those names may themselves contain information you consider private. Review reports and screenshots before sharing them.

Turnover Guard withholds obvious absolute filesystem paths and internal exceptions from its customer-facing report/UI contracts, but it does not claim that native AAF private metadata produced by Adobe is universally free of source references. Inspect exported material before external delivery.

Support email

If you email turnoverguard.support@gmail.com, your message and the information you choose to include are processed through the email provider used for that mailbox. Support messages may be retained as reasonably needed to respond, troubleshoot, maintain a support history, or meet legal obligations. Avoid sending private source media, passwords, payment-card details, full production projects or unnecessary personal information.

You may contact the same address to ask about support data you previously supplied or to request deletion where applicable.

Website hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web-request and service data under its own terms and privacy practices. Turnover Guard does not add its own analytics or tracking scripts to this site.

Purchases

Turnover Guard is planned for distribution through Adobe Marketplace. Purchase, entitlement, tax, payment and refund information is handled by the authorized marketplace/merchant flow rather than by this static website. Do not send payment-card information to the Turnover Guard support email.

Changes

If Turnover Guard later adds materially different data practices, this page should be updated before those practices are introduced where notice or consent is required. A software or policy update does not remove rights that cannot lawfully be waived.
