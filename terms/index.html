What Turnover Guard does not guarantee

Turnover Guard checks common pre-export conditions; it cannot identify every editing, media, exporter or interchange problem. Keep project/media backups and inspect the resulting turnover before delivery.

  • Nested/multicam contents are not recursively scanned in v0.1.
  • Through-edit inference is experimental and off by default.
  • Locate/navigation is not offered in v0.1.
