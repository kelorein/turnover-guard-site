Help with Turnover Guard

Version 0.1.0 · Supported target: Windows + Premiere Pro 26.3.2

Need help?
Email turnoverguard.support@gmail.com. For faster troubleshooting, include the Turnover Guard version, Premiere version/build, what you clicked, the visible message, and a small disposable reproduction when possible. Do not send private media, passwords or payment-card details.

Basic workflow

  1. Open the sequence you intend to turn over.
  2. Open Turnover Guard and choose Run Preflight.
  3. Resolve blockers in Premiere. Review warnings deliberately; acknowledgement means reviewed, not repaired.
  4. Physically verify audio track locks in Premiere before making the manual unlocked-track confirmation. Turnover Guard does not detect track-lock state.
  5. Choose a local parent folder when prompted for output.
  6. Request AAF — Separate WAV export and preserve the entire generated turnover folder.
  7. Import and review the package in the receiving workflow before delivery.

What the result labels mean

  • Blocker: a condition that must be resolved before export.
  • Warning: a condition that requires editorial review. Acknowledging it does not lower its severity.
  • Manual Check: a human confirmation or required observation that cannot be inferred automatically.
  • Info: context or an experimental suggestion that does not block by itself.
  • Passed: an applicable category completed without a finding or required missing observation. It is not a guarantee that the receiving application will reproduce the turnover perfectly.

Common questions

Why did my lock confirmation disappear?

That is expected after a new scan, sequence change, stale result, or an export attempt that consumes the confirmation. Recheck the track locks in Premiere before confirming again.

Why is export disabled?

Read the reason shown in the panel. Typical causes include blockers, incomplete required observations, unreviewed warnings, missing manual confirmation, no eligible audio, or no destination. Required unknowns cannot be bypassed by acknowledgement.

Why did the panel reset when I switched sequences?

Resetting old review state is a safe freshness behavior. Review and confirmation from one sequence must not transfer to another.

What does AAF — Separate WAV guarantee?

It describes the export package only. It does not certify Pro Tools, Resolve, or any other receiving application. Panning, routing, effect reconstruction, audible equivalence and universal AAF compatibility are not guaranteed.

Does Turnover Guard scan inside nested or multicam items?

No. Version 0.1.0 warns about top-level nested/multicam items but does not recursively qualify their contents or angles.

Does it have Locate/navigation?

No. Locate/navigation is not offered in v0.1. Use the displayed track/clip context to inspect the item manually in Premiere.

Supported and unqualified environments

Public support for v0.1 is intentionally narrow: Windows + Premiere Pro 26.3.2. Other Premiere versions, macOS, fully disconnected host sessions, OS permission-denied environments, unusual service failures, disk-full conditions and arbitrary crash/power-loss scenarios are unqualified.
